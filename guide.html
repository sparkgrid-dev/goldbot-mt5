User Guide

Everything you need to install Gold Bot, understand every setting, and run it safely. For version 1.6.

Read this firstGold Bot is experimental. In our own 90-day tests on real gold ticks the strategy lost money overall. Use a demo account. Nothing here is financial advice.

1. Before you start

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit). The bot itself does not run on Mac, iPhone or Android (but you can view it on your phone, see Phone access).
  • MetaTrader 5 (MT5) from your broker, installed and logged in. Start with a demo account.
  • The gold symbol (XAUUSD or GOLD) visible in MT5's Market Watch. The bot finds it automatically.
  • Keep the PC on and MT5 open while the bot trades. Sleep mode stops it.

2. Install & first run

  1. Download GoldBot.exe from the home page and save it anywhere, for example your Desktop.
  2. Open MT5, log in to your demo account and click Algo Trading in the toolbar so it turns green.
  3. Double-click GoldBot.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. The app isn't code-signed; you can compare its fingerprint with the one on the home page first.
  4. A black window opens (keep it open: closing it stops the bot) and your browser opens the dashboard at http://127.0.0.1:8790.
  5. Check the settings (section 5), then press Start trading. Trading is always off when the app starts.
Sized to your account automaticallyOn the very first start, the bot reads your account balance and leverage and applies the matching account-size preset (section 5). The Activity log shows what it chose, for example "Settings sized for a $100 account (first start): 1 x 0.01 lots, target $0.50, stop $20 per batch, daily loss limit $20, recovery off, counter off." It's only a starting point: change anything you like.
TipOnly one copy of the bot can run at a time. If you double-click it again, the second window just says it's already running.

4. How it trades

At a set second of every 1-minute (M1) candle, usually second 30, the bot looks at the candle:

  • Direction: by default it follows the candle: green → BUY, red → SELL.
  • Filters: it only enters if the candle has moved enough, the wick isn't too long, and the trend / M5-candle rules you ticked agree.
  • Exit (simple mode, the default): one trade at a time, closed at its stop, at the trailing profit, or at the chosen second of the candle.
  • Exit (basket mode): opens a batch of trades together; each trade closes at its own small target at the broker; the batch has one shared stop. Optional recovery and counter batches (section 5).

Every trade gets a stop and target stored at your broker, so trades stay protected even if the bot or PC is closed.

5. Settings explained

Money amounts are in your account currency. "Gold move" means dollars of gold price (at 0.01 lots, a $1 gold move ≈ $1 profit or loss per trade; at 0.10 lots ≈ $10).

Account-size presets and risk warnings

A ready-made starting point, not a lockPresets give you a tested setup to try for your account size. After applying one you can still change every setting by hand and experiment with your own ideas.
Account size presetAt the top of Settings, pick an amount: $100 (the minimum), $200, $300, $400, $500, $750, $1,000 and up, My balance, or Other amount… ($100 or more). A preview shows trades × lots, target and stop per batch, daily limit, recovery, counter batches and the gold move to the stop. Press Apply (it asks first). Your entry rules stay as they are.
Risk warningsA red box appears when settings risk too much for the balance: daily loss limit above 25% of the account, stop above 20% per batch (10% per trade in simple mode), one batch needing more than half the balance as margin, or unlimited recovery batches.

$100 to $199: these accounts start from the developer's own tested $100 setup. Pick the $100 preset (or My balance) to see its exact numbers in the preview before you apply it. At low leverage (for example 1:100) the margin only carries 1 trade, so it uses 1 trade and turns recovery and counter batches off.

How the presets are calculated from $200 up: every size is scaled from the developer's tested $10,000 setup: 5 trades × 0.02 lots, each trade targeting $0.50 of gold, a stop of 20% of the balance per batch, a daily loss limit of 20%, recovery every $3 of gold, and counter batches targeting $4 of gold.

  • Exposure, stop and daily limit grow in proportion to the amount ($200 → $40 stop and daily limit; $1,000 → $200; $10,000 → $2,000).
  • The smallest trade is 0.01 lots, so smaller accounts use fewer trades per batch (1 trade up to about $1,000, 2 at $2,000, 5 from about $5,000).
  • Your account's leverage decides how many recovery and counter batches the margin can safely carry (one batch uses at most ~30% of the balance, everything together ~60%). At 1:100 a $100 account gets no recovery; at 1:1000 it can.
NoteThe $10,000 template uses a large 20% stop and daily limit. That is the developer's own experimental choice, not a recommendation. Many traders risk far less, for example 1–5% per trade. Lower the stop and daily limit if you prefer.

5b. Small accounts (for example $100)

All the settings above are on the dashboard, so you can set the bot up for any account size. Small accounts need much smaller numbers, because gold moves fast and the default settings are sized for larger accounts.

Why small accounts need care At 0.01 lots, a $10 move in gold is about $10 per trade. Gold often moves $10–20 in an hour. Each 0.01-lot gold trade also ties up roughly $30–40 of margin (at 1:100 leverage), so a $100 account can only hold 2–3 trades at once. Batches of 5 or recovery batches can trigger a broker stop-out that closes everything at a large loss. Small accounts have been wiped this way.

The easiest way: open a $100 demo account and choose the $100 preset (or let the bot size itself on first start). If you'd like to be more careful than the preset, a gentler setup for about $100 looks like this (an example, not advice):

6. Safety features

  • Demo only by default. On a live account the bot refuses to trade (see section 8).
  • Broker-side stops and targets on every trade.
  • Daily loss limit (losses only) closes everything and stops for the day.
  • Restarting never closes trades. The bot finds them again; press Start to keep managing them.
  • One copy only, so two bots can't fight over the same trades.
  • Slipped fills fixed: if the broker fills at a different price, the stop and target move to the real fill; a warning appears in Activity if that fails.

7. Phone access (iPhone & Android)

The bot keeps running on your PC; your phone opens the same dashboard privately through Tailscale, a free private network between your own devices. Nothing is put on the public internet.

Reading this on your PC?Scan this code with your phone to open these steps there, so you can follow along on both devices.
  1. Set a PIN: on the PC dashboard, Settings → Phone access, enter a 6–12 digit PIN twice, Save PIN.
  2. Install Tailscale on the PC (tailscale.com/download) and on your phone (App Store / Google Play). Sign in with the same account on both.
  3. Share the dashboard on your tailnet: on the PC, open PowerShell and run: 
    tailscale serve --bg 8790
    If Tailscale asks you to enable HTTPS for your network, follow its link and allow it. It prints an address like https://your-pc.your-tailnet.ts.net.
  4. On your phone (Tailscale switched on), open that address, enter your PIN.
  5. Add to Home Screen: iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install app. You get the gold "Au" app icon.
Good to knowThe phone stays logged in for 30 days (use Log out on the phone to end it). 5 wrong PINs lock logins for 60 seconds. The PIN can only be changed on the PC. Your phone can reach only your PC, and only while the bot is running.

8. Live trading (at your own risk)

⚠ Real money: please read carefully

  • In our own 90-day tests the strategy lost money overall. Short winning streaks are common and misleading.
  • Recovery and basket modes can turn a small loss into a very large one within minutes. Real accounts have been wiped this way.
  • Brokers may restrict or close accounts with very frequent automated orders.
  • You alone are responsible for every trade the bot places. This is not financial advice and there is no guarantee of profit.

Only consider live trading after weeks of stable demo results, with money you can afford to lose completely. If you still choose to, this is how to unlock it:

  1. Close the bot (close its black window).
  2. Press Win + R, type %LOCALAPPDATA%\GoldBot and press Enter.
  3. Open config.json with Notepad.
  4. Change "allow_live": false to "allow_live": true and save.
  5. Start the bot again. The account tile shows a red LIVE badge.

Before pressing Start on a live account: lot 0.01, a small daily loss limit, a small stop per batch, and recovery off or max 1 batch. To lock it again, set allow_live back to false.

10. Update, reset, uninstall

  • Update: download the latest GoldBot.exe from the home page and replace the old file. Your settings and history are kept.
  • Reset settings: close the bot and delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\GoldBot\config.json; it is recreated with defaults.
  • Uninstall: delete GoldBot.exe and the %LOCALAPPDATA%\GoldBot folder. Nothing else is installed. If you set up phone access, you can also run tailscale serve reset.
