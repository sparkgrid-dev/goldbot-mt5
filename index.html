How it works

Up and running in three steps

No coding and no Python. Just MetaTrader 5 and one app.

1

Install

Open MT5 on a demo account, turn on Algo Trading, and run GoldBot.exe.

2

Pick a setup

Choose your account size ($100, $200, $300 …). The settings are scaled for you, and every value stays editable.

3

Watch it trade

Press Start. Follow every decision live on the dashboard or on your phone.

Features

Everything in one dashboard

Runs entirely on your own computer. Nothing is sent to us.

M1 candle entries

Decides at a set second of each candle using candle colour, M1/M5 trends and a wick filter.

Basket trading

Batches of trades with their own broker targets, plus optional recovery and counter batches.

Broker-side protection

Stops and targets live at your broker, so trades stay protected if the app closes.

Daily loss limit

Counts losses only. When reached, it closes everything and stops until tomorrow.

Live dashboard

Results, open and closed trades with times, an activity log, history and every setting in one page.

Phone app

Open your dashboard on iPhone or Android through your own private Tailscale network, PIN-protected.

Fits any account

Ready-made setups from $100 with a live preview. Use one as a start, then tweak anything.

Demo-first safety

Trades demo accounts by default, warns when settings risk too much, and only one copy can run.

Install

Four steps, about five minutes

You need MetaTrader 5 from your broker. Start with a demo account. Full details in the User guide.

1

MetaTrader 5

Install MT5 from your broker, log in to a demo account and add gold to Market Watch.

2

Algo Trading on

Click Algo Trading in MT5's toolbar so it turns green. Keep MT5 open.

3

Run GoldBot.exe

Double-click it. At "Windows protected your PC", choose More info → Run anyway.

4

Pick & press Start

The dashboard opens at 127.0.0.1:8790. Choose a setup and press Start trading.

FAQ

Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

The app isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen warns about any new download. Check the fingerprint below matches, then choose More info → Run anyway.

Can I use it with $100?

Yes. Pick the $100 setup in Settings (the minimum). It uses one 0.01-lot trade at a time with a stop and daily limit scaled to the account. Try it on a $100 demo account first.

Can I trade a live account?

Not by default: the bot refuses live accounts. You can unlock it yourself at your own risk; the steps and warnings are in the User guide, section 8.

Does closing the app close my trades?

No. Open trades stay in MetaTrader 5 with their stops and targets at the broker. Restarting never closes trades.

How do I uninstall?

Delete GoldBot.exe and the %LOCALAPPDATA%\GoldBot folder. Nothing else is installed.
