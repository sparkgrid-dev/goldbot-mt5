What it does

Everything runs on your own computer. Nothing is sent to us.

M1 candle entries

Decides at a set second of each 1-minute candle using candle colour, M1/M5 trends and a wick filter.

Basket trading

Opens a batch of trades together, each with its own target at the broker, and an optional recovery and counter-batch mode.

Broker-side protection

Stops and targets are stored at your broker, so trades stay protected even if the app or your PC is closed.

Daily loss limit

Counts losses only. When reached it closes everything and stops trading until the next day.

Live dashboard

Results, open and closed trades with entry and exit times, an activity log with history, and all settings in one page.

Phone app

Open your dashboard on iPhone or Android through your own private Tailscale network, protected by a PIN, and add it to your home screen.

Trade history

Every closed trade with entry and exit times and prices, grouped by day for the last 30 days.

Demo-first

Trades demo accounts only out of the box. Live trading stays locked unless you deliberately unlock it.

Install in 4 steps

You need a MetaTrader 5 terminal from your broker. A demo account is strongly recommended.

Install MetaTrader 5 and log in

Download MT5 from your broker, log in to a demo account and add the gold symbol (XAUUSD / GOLD) to Market Watch.

Turn on Algo Trading

In MT5 click the Algo Trading button in the toolbar so it turns green. Keep MT5 open while the bot runs.

Download and run GoldBot.exe

Save GoldBot.exe anywhere (e.g. your Desktop) and double-click it. If Windows SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway (the app is not code-signed). Keep its window open.

Open the dashboard and press Start

Your browser opens http://127.0.0.1:8790. Check the settings, then press Start trading. Trading is always off after the app starts.

FAQ

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

The app isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen warns about any new download. Check the file's fingerprint below matches before running it, then choose More info → Run anyway.

Where are my settings stored?

In %LOCALAPPDATA%\GoldBot (config, activity history and trade log). Delete that folder to reset everything.

Can I trade a live account?

Not by default: the bot refuses live accounts. Unlocking it means editing allow_live in the config file yourself. Please test on demo first.

Does closing the app close my trades?

No. Open trades stay in MetaTrader 5 with their stops and targets at the broker. Restarting the app never closes trades.

How do I uninstall?

Delete GoldBot.exe and the %LOCALAPPDATA%\GoldBot folder. Nothing else is installed.
